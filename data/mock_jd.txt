SENIOR PRODUCT DATA ANALYST
Harborline Health — Chicago, IL or Remote (United States)

ABOUT HARBORLINE HEALTH

Harborline Health is a fictional B2B healthcare software company that helps
outpatient care teams coordinate referrals, scheduling, and patient follow-up.
Our product organization is expanding its analytics practice so that product,
engineering, operations, and clinical partners can make faster, evidence-based
decisions.

THE ROLE

We are hiring a Senior Product Data Analyst to own analytics for our Patient
Access product area. You will partner with a product manager, engineering lead,
designer, and operations stakeholders. The successful candidate will convert
ambiguous product questions into measurable hypotheses, trustworthy metrics,
and clear recommendations.

WHAT YOU WILL DO

- Build reproducible analyses and decision-support datasets using advanced SQL
  and Python.
- Define product metrics and create self-service dashboards in Looker or a
  comparable business intelligence platform.
- Design and analyze A/B tests, quasi-experiments, and funnel analyses; explain
  assumptions and limitations to non-technical partners.
- Partner with product managers to translate ambiguous business questions into
  measurable hypotheses and analysis plans.
- Present recommendations to product, engineering, operations, and executive
  stakeholders, including when the data does not support the proposed idea.
- Improve analytics reliability through version control, peer review,
  documentation, and automated data-quality checks.
- Mentor analysts on experimental design, metric definition, and stakeholder
  communication.

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

- 5+ years of professional experience in product analytics, data analytics, or
  a closely related role, including at least 2 years supporting a digital
  product team.
- Advanced SQL skills, including complex joins, window functions, and query
  optimization on a cloud data warehouse such as BigQuery or Snowflake.
- Proficiency in Python for analysis, automation, or statistical modeling.
- Hands-on experience designing experiments and interpreting statistical
  results for product decisions.
- Demonstrated ability to communicate a complex analysis as a concise business
  recommendation for both technical and non-technical audiences.

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

- Experience with dbt, Airflow, or another analytics engineering or workflow
  orchestration tool.
- Experience working with healthcare, regulated data, or privacy-sensitive
  products.
- Familiarity with causal inference methods beyond standard randomized tests.

SUCCESS IN THE FIRST SIX MONTHS

- Establish a trusted activation funnel and metric definitions for Patient
  Access.
- Deliver at least one experiment or quasi-experiment that changes a product
  decision.
- Reduce recurring manual analysis by publishing reusable datasets and
  self-service reporting.

HIRING PROCESS

The interview process includes a recruiter conversation, a hiring-manager
discussion, a practical analytics case, and a cross-functional panel. Harborline
Health is an equal opportunity employer. This teaching example is fictional and
does not represent a real company or open role.
